    How to play

    1. 👀
      Look around. You're dropped somewhere on or near the UC Berkeley campus. Drag to look and click the arrows to walk.
    2. 🗺️
      Open the map. Hover the map in the corner (or tap Map on mobile) to make it bigger, then click where you think you are.
    3. 📍
      Lock it in. Press Guess! (or Space). The closer you are, the more points, up to 5,000 per spot.
    4. 📒
      Collect stickers. Score 2,500+ on a landmark to add it to your Sticker Book.
    🔔 Campus Classic Famous landmarks, 5 rounds, no rush.
    🧭 Wander Mode Random streets around campus. Tougher!
    ⏱️ Bear Sprint Landmarks against a 45-second clock.
    📅 Daily Stroll The same 5 spots for everyone today. Share your score!

    Keys: Space guess / next · R back to start · H hint · M toggle map
